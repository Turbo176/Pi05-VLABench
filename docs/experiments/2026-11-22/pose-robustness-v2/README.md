## Dataset-to-controller replay precision

The official policy adapter replay reached success on one training episode of each task: fruit seed 42 in 132 controls, flower seed 42 in 179, and fridge seed 43 in 479. The adapter modifies float32 action positions in place when adding the robot origin; the direct source replay performs that addition in float64. The difference in the fridge probe was at most `5.84e-8` m at a requested target. After contact interactions, the adapter run differed from stored fridge states by up to 1.52 mm in position and 0.00541 rad in orientation, and the most different sampled camera frame had mean absolute error 11.18/255. The successful adapter replay is therefore not pixel-identical to the source replay. The original strict drift checks failed and are preserved in logs; `--report-drift` records the mismatch while still requiring task success.

An independent direct replay of the exact stored fridge controls matched all sampled camera pixels exactly and measured source state within float32 storage error (`1.19e-7`). These measurements distinguish action rounding and contact sensitivity from a systematic camera-order or timestamp mismatch. All learned-policy stages continue using the unchanged official adapter.
